Step 1 · 16-pin ESP32-CAM (OV2640) capture

Eight orbital nodes, 45° apart at 2.6 m, each capturing a 1600×1200 OV2640 frame. Field wiring uses only 5V and GND; GPIO 33 is the countdown LED and GPIO 4 the flash LED. Live nodes POST to /api/esp32/upload and poll /api/esp32/command every 3 s.

Step 2 · COLMAP 3D reconstruction

Native colmap is used when the binary is present (feature_extractor → exhaustive_matcher → mapper); otherwise the hybrid engine runs OpenCV SIFT + RANSAC epipolar matching and an incremental PnP solver, then similarity-aligns the model to the surveyed rig to recover metric scale.

Step 3 · AI defect analysis & report

Multi-stage OpenCV detection — scale-space local contrast (cracks), HSV hue/red-dominance (corrosion), aggregate texture energy (spalling) and neutral local brightening (efflorescence) — followed by ACI 224R / ACI 201.1R severity grading and a structural health index.

Step 4 · 2D→3D ray back-projection

Each 2D defect centroid is back-projected as a world ray d = RTK-1[u,v,1]T, intersected with the fitted cylinder, then re-solved by least-squares triangulation over all contributing views to give a metric 3D point with an uncertainty.
